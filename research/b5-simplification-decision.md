**Status date:** 2026-09-17

the exhaustive geometric profile check with local and axis formulas.

or an additional residue-label search as proof inputs.

fallback to one denominator-cleared scalar inequality.
Thus the retained computation needs only a generator and two elementary
integer predicates, not certificate discovery or certificate data.

saved 431-shape fallback list. It is useful evidence for the possible
analytic theorem that genuine B5 ideals satisfy \(U_2\ge m\).

classification of every possible local failure. Replacing the axis
predicate with that probe would add a computation rather than remove one.

215, 212, 3, and 1 shapes at corners \((1,1,3),(1,2,2),(1,1,4),(1,2,3)\).
This is a diagnostic of a proposed replacement, not R5 or its domain coverage.
No uniform charging/classification argument has been obtained within this
gate. This is not a claim that an analytic proof is impossible.

The gate is closed with the finite route retained. R5 must independently
regenerate the whole B5.4 family, check both formulas, audit integer
bounds, and reject any shape where both predicates fail.
