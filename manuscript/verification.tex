\section{Exact finite verification}\label{sec:verification}

The accompanying repository provides source code and certificates for
the seven finite assertions in Section~\ref{sec:proof}. Their domains
and acceptance inequalities are stated in the corresponding finite
lemmas. The computations enumerate a bounded generator family and two
families of plane profiles, evaluate the finite degree strip, and
establish three interval bounds on real parameter regions.

Each assertion is verified by two separate implementations using
different representations or evaluation strategies. They independently
generate the relevant families and recompute the required bounds using
exact integer or rational arithmetic. The two explicit short-corner
witnesses are also checked directly.

The interval certificates describe finite subdivisions of closed
parameter regions. After the mathematically justified tightening from
Section~\ref{sec:proof}, each terminal region is proved empty, handled
by an applicable analytic estimate, or bounded by a complete evaluation
of the horn recurrence. The checkers verify the subdivision's coverage,
including shared boundaries, and recompute every terminal bound.
The certificates establish bounds throughout the closed real parameter
regions.

A complete run of both implementations has verified all seven finite
assertions. The repository documentation gives execution instructions,
algorithm descriptions, arithmetic safeguards, and the complete-run
record.
